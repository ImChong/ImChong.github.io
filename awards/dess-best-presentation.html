Best Presentation Award — DESS

ASME Dayton Section · November 2018

Award: Best Presentation Award

Event: Dayton Engineering Science Symposium (DESS), sponsored by the ASME Dayton Section

Date: November 2018

Recipient: Chong Liu (刘冲)

Affiliation at the time: Senior mechanical engineering major, Miami University College of Engineering and Computing

Research Presented

The award-winning presentation was titled "Machining of Blind and High Aspect Ratio Through Micro-holes on Metallic Glass by Micro EDM", by Chong Liu and Muhammad P. Jahan, Department of Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering, Miami University.

The presentation title slide, as published with the original Miami University news story.

News Coverage

Miami University's Science Friday Research News covered the award on 11/09/2018 under the headline "Senior mechanical engineering major won the Best Presentation Award at the Dayton Engineering Science Symposium (DESS)". The entry reads:

"Chong Liu, won the Best Presentation Award at the Dayton Engineering Science Symposium (DESS) for his research on Micro Electrical Discharge Machining (EDM)."

最佳演讲奖 — 代顿工程科学研讨会 (DESS)

ASME 代顿分部 · 2018年11月

奖项：最佳演讲奖（Best Presentation Award）

会议：代顿工程科学研讨会（Dayton Engineering Science Symposium, DESS），由 ASME 代顿分部主办

时间：2018年11月

获奖人：刘冲

当时身份：迈阿密大学工程与计算机学院机械工程专业大四学生

研究内容

获奖演讲题目为 "Machining of Blind and High Aspect Ratio Through Micro-holes on Metallic Glass by Micro EDM"（微电火花加工金属玻璃盲孔与高深径比通孔），作者为刘冲与 Muhammad P. Jahan，单位为迈阿密大学机械与制造工程系。

演讲标题页，取自迈阿密大学原始新闻报道所配图片。

"Chong Liu, won the Best Presentation Award at the Dayton Engineering Science Symposium (DESS) for his research on Micro Electrical Discharge Machining (EDM)."
