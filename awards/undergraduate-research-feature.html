Featured Undergraduate Researcher

Miami University College of Engineering and Computing · August 2018

Recognition: Featured undergraduate researcher

Published by: Miami University College of Engineering and Computing

Date: 21 August 2018

Subject: Chong Liu (刘冲)

Topic: Micro-electrical discharge machining (micro-EDM) of Bulk Metallic Glass

The Research

The feature covered undergraduate research into the behaviour of Bulk Metallic Glass (BMG) under micro-electrical discharge machining — the same line of work that later won the Best Presentation Award at the Dayton Engineering Science Symposium.

In the lab at the micro-EDM machine, as published with the original Miami University news story.

Related: Best Presentation Award — DESS →

News Coverage

Miami University's Science Friday Research News carried the story on 08/21/2018 under the headline "Student performs micro-electrical discharge machining on Bulk Metallic Glass". The entry reads:

"A senior mechanical engineering major is studying the behavior of Bulk Metallic Glass through the use of micro-electrical discharge machining."

研究方向：块体金属玻璃（BMG）的微电火花加工（micro-EDM）

研究内容

该报道介绍了本科阶段关于块体金属玻璃（Bulk Metallic Glass, BMG）在微电火花加工条件下材料行为的研究工作 —— 这一研究方向随后在代顿工程科学研讨会上获得最佳演讲奖。

实验室中操作微电火花加工设备，取自迈阿密大学原始新闻报道所配图片。

相关：最佳演讲奖 — 代顿工程科学研讨会 (DESS) →
